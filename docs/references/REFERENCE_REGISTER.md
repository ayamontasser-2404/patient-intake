## Evidence handling

For every downloaded reference:
- Record exact title, publisher/author, URL, access date, and version/date printed on document.
- Record licence or permission status.
- Keep a local copy only when permitted.
- Do not upload real patient examples.
- Use synthetic documents for OCR testing.
- Do not imply a sample practice form is mandatory across Germany.

## Product design caveat

The prototype's form is inspired by common fields in public practice questionnaires. It is not a copy of a particular practice's form and must be configurable for a real practice's workflow.
